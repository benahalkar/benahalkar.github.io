I’m an engineer passionate about building reliable solutions that blend innovative design with robust engineering for broader global impact. My favorite work lies at the intersection of ambition and practicality, creating experiences that not only push the boundaries of current technology but are meticulously built for performance and user experience.

I am currently working at Capital One as an AI Engineer on Behavioral Foundational Models (BFMs), specifically on research, deployment, and inference optimizations. I attended Columbia University for my graduate studies, focused on Deep Learning and Distributed and Edge Computing, graduating with Honors and receiving the Master of Excellence award. Some of my work with professors included conducting research under Dr. Micah Goldblum on multi-modal LLMs, helping Dr. Helen H. Lu with bio-chemical measurements, and working under IBM’s Dr. Kaoutar El Maghraoui on IBM foundation stack models.

In the past, I’ve had the opportunity to develop end-to-end software across a variety of settings — from real-time streaming datacorps and large sensor distribution corporations to industrial automation start-ups and electric racecar teams. Additionally, I also released source code and design files for tools that help automate mundane tasks, and make life easier.

Outside of work, I enjoy cooking, painting, practicing calligraphy, and exploring the city. Having lived in Mumbai and New York, I’m never short of options for new experiences.
