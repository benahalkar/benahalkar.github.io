### PoseWise: Yoga Mentor

**Repository:** [github.com/benahalkar/3D-pose-estimation-and-correction](https://github.com/benahalkar/3D-pose-estimation-and-correction)

This project focuses on real-time 3D pose estimation and correction on yoga positions. The goal is to develop a vectorized algorithm capable of accurately estimating and correcting yoga poses in real time, enabling users to receive immediate feedback on their posture during yoga practice.

The aim was to deploy a compute- and time-intensive multi-thread UI application on a limited-compute, limited-power mobile edge device.

Technologies used: PyTorch, Jetson NANO, CUDA, OpenCV, Tkinter, Docker.
