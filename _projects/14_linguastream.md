### LinguaStream: Multilingual Streaming Translator

**Repository:** [github.com/benahalkar/Multilingual-Streaming-Translator](https://github.com/benahalkar/Multilingual-Streaming-Translator)

An Apache Beam-based streaming pipeline that leverages Meta's NLLB LLM to perform one-to-many language translation.

Windowing and chunking strategies were created to maximize performance measured using Euclidean distance and BLEU score metrics.

Technologies used: Apache Beam, HuggingFace, Kafka, PubSub, Google Cloud.
