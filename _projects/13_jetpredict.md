**Repository:** [github.com/benahalkar/Flight-data-analytics](https://github.com/benahalkar/Flight-data-analytics)

This project presents a real-time weather visualization and risk assessment tool for flights, leveraging and processing historical weather and bulk flight data to ensure safer operations by highlighting potential weather-related risks along flight routes.

Technologies used: PySpark, Google Cloud, Airflow, Plotly, REST APIs.
