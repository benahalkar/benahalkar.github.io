### OfferCompass: Recommendation System for Offers

**Repository:** [github.com/benahalkar/Merchant-recommendation-system](https://github.com/benahalkar/Merchant-recommendation-system)

This project analyzes historical and current credit card transactions to recommend new, personalized offers to loyal customers.

Performed extensive feature engineering using RFA, RFE, and Boruta and modeled the data on Gradient Boosting models to be evaluated on RMSE.

Technologies used: Jupyter, Sklearn, XGBoost, Boruta, RFA-RFE, Matplotlib, Seaborn.
